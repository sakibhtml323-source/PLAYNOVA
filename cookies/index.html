Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

This policy explains how cookies and browser storage are used on PLAYNOVA.

What we use

  • localStorage (not a cookie, but similar in effect): game high scores, favorites, recently-played list, play counts, audio preferences. All stored only on your device.
  • localStorage (continued): your PLAYNOVA profile (display name, avatar, XP, level, virtual coins, achievements, owned cosmetics, daily-challenge state, settings) and per-game saved data, stored under keys such as pn_data and pn_gls:<game>.
  • IndexedDB (database playnova): a backup copy of your profile so it can be restored if the browser clears localStorage. Stays on your device.
  • Service worker cache (cache names starting with playnova-): copies of pages and game files so visited pages can open again offline. Stays on your device.
  • No tracking cookies are set by PLAYNOVA itself, and PLAYNOVA does not send your profile or game data to any server.

Data kept in your browser can be viewed, edited or deleted by you (browser settings, or Profile → Settings → Reset). Because it is stored locally, virtual XP and coins are not protected against editing and have no cash value.

Advertising cookies

If the owner enables an ad provider (for example Google AdSense, Adsterra, Monetag or PropellerAds), that provider may set cookies or use device identifiers for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. Advertising is currently disabled; when enabled, a consent notice will be shown where required by law (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR, ePrivacy Directive).

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. The games remain fully playable without them (only high scores/favorites are lost).
