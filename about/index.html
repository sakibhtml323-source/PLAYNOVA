About Us

Last updated: 2026-11-04

PLAYNOVA is an independent gaming portal built around one idea: quantity without sameness. Every one of the 500 games has its own core mechanic — no reskins, no clones with a new coat of paint.

What makes the collection different

  • 500 mechanically unique games — match-3, tower defense, idle sims, quiz shows, racers, roguelikes and dozens of other genres.
  • Self-contained games — each game is a single HTML file that loads instantly and works offline after first visit.
  • Original artwork — every game has a unique banner and thumbnail, generated as original artwork for this portal.
  • Original audio — the portal's background music is generated live with WebAudio; no licensed tracks required.
  • Privacy-first — no accounts, no server-side tracking; your favorites and scores stay on your device.
  • No Google Ads — advertising, when enabled by the owner, uses independent networks (for example Google AdSense, Adsterra, Monetag, PropellerAds) in clearly separated banner slots.

Technology

Plain HTML5 canvas + JavaScript, a hand-built design system, a modular fullscreen game player, and a provider-agnostic advertising layer. No frameworks, no build step required to deploy.
