Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

PLAYNOVA ("the Site") is a free browser-games portal. This policy explains what data is handled when you use the Site.

Data we store on your device

  • Game progress and high scores — each game may save scores/settings in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Favorites, recently-played and play counts — stored in localStorage to personalize the Site. Clearing browser data removes them.
  • Audio preferences — volume/mute settings stored in localStorage.

Data we do not collect

We do not run accounts, do not require personal information, and do not operate our own analytics or tracking servers. No advertising is shown right now and no ad network is loaded.

Analytics

If the site owner later enables Google Analytics 4, it would collect anonymized usage statistics (pages visited, device type). No advertising identifiers are used.

Advertising

The Site includes a modular advertising system supporting Google AdSense, Adsterra, Monetag and/or PropellerAds. Advertising is disabled until the site owner configures a provider. If enabled, the chosen third-party provider may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to that provider's own privacy policy. Ad slots are never placed over gameplay or controls.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy.

Third-party content

Games are served from this domain only. No third-party game iframes or embeds are used.

Your rights & contact

Because we hold no server-side personal data, "deletion" is as simple as clearing your browser storage. Questions: Contact Us.
